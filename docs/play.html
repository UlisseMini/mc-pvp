Fight the bots, practice in slow motion, and inspect your replays

Half the reason I built this lab was to have a sandbox for actually improving at 1.8.9 PvP—drilling jump resets, blockhitting, KB braking, and fighting calibrated RL bots on the real 13×13 Minemen Club sumo floor without waiting in queues.

1. Classic 1.8.9 Practice Lab (macOS)

You need Python 3, a Java 17 JDK, and the official Minecraft launcher with Minecraft Java 1.8.9 installed. From the repo folder: 

python3 lab.py prepare
python3 lab.py build
python3 lab.py sandbox-test
python3 lab.py serve --bot-backend legacy

Open Minecraft 1.8.9 → Multiplayer → Direct Connect → 127.0.0.1:25565 (or double-click Start PvP Lab.command on macOS for the Forge bot backend). Useful in-game commands:

2. Slow-Motion Sumo (0.10× to 1.00× speed)

Double-click Start Slow Sumo.command (or run python3 slow_sumo.py 0.5) and connect to 127.0.0.1:25585 with the Forge 1.8.9 practice mod. At half speed, every physics tick lasts 100 real ms, a 10-tick fresh-hit window lasts 1 full second, bot CPS scales proportionally, and simulated ping scales too. Press Esc → Slow motion or type /slowmo 0.35 to change speed mid-duel.

3. Forge 1.8.9 Practice Mod Highlights

Install with python3 practice-client/build.py --install, then launch Forge 1.8.9. Type /p for the clickable in-game menu:

  • Sumo Coach (/p sumocoach): Live green crosshair flash + sound cues when you land verified 0.6× KB brakes, Sprint KB vs. Base KB verdict under the crosshair after each fresh hit, and post-match "biggest slides" cards showing where a missed brake cost you blocks.
  • Two-POV Reach & Ghosts: /p reach on (translucent reach spheres), /p ghost me on (pink silhouette showing where the bot's delayed client sees you), /p range both on (inner blue / outer red crosshair brackets), and live numeric reach advantage.
  • Offline Multi-POV Replay Viewer (/p replays or F8): Reconstructs recorded lab and opt-in remote captures completely offline with 0.1×–4× scrubbing, single-tick stepping (, / .), live recorded keystrokes, KB-reduction timeline marks, and first-person / spectator cameras.
  • Neural BowAim & BedWars Overlays: /p bowaim on (trajectory arc, K=6 predicted ghosts, hit chance & V_wait indicator) and /p forecast (3D volumetric heatmaps & ghosts).

4. Modern Sword Lab (Java 26.3) & Tests

Double-click Start Modern Sword Lab.command to fight the Java 26.3 bots on 127.0.0.1:25663 (bots run inside a headless Lima Linux VM on macOS), or Watch Sword League.command for the 60fps exhibition viewer.

python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
